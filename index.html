WHY THIS IS THE
QUESTION OF POWER

For millennia, women have been constrained by oppressive customs, restrictive laws and disproportionate social expectations. Central to this subordination is the enduring construction of women as the “weaker sex”, fragile and in need of protection, a notion that paradoxically legitimised male control. Although the expansion of human rights discourse has since given women greater scope for advocacy, a structural imbalance persists: power in parliaments, corporate boardrooms and most decision-making spaces remains predominantly male.

This imbalance illustrates why the central question of gender equality is, at its core, a question of power: who holds it, who is excluded from it, and who has the authority to redistribute it. Substantive change therefore requires not only the efforts of those seeking access, but also the cooperation of those who already hold power. Accordingly, contemporary feminism depends on the engagement of progressive men willing to cede decision-making space and use their influence to amplify women’s voices. Gender equality, the core objective of SDG 5, should thus be understood not as a women’s issue but as a collective societal responsibility. Hence the call to action:

"Pass the mic.Nominate a woman.Change who decides."

ABOUT THE
ARTWORK

The Weave of Voices

Sub-Call to action

"Use your voice to lift another."

Inspiration

Thân em như tấm lụa đào
Phất phơ giữa chợ biết vào tay ai?

“My self is like a bolt of pink silk, fluttering in the marketplace — into whose hands will it fall?”

Based on this sub-call to action and inspired by this Vietnamese folk verse, the generative artwork reimagines silk, once a metaphor for women's vulnerability and lack of agency under patriarchy, as a symbol of collective empowerment.

Each thread represents an individual voice, retaining its identity as it intertwines with others to form something stronger. Through this weaving, the artwork celebrates solidarity as a way for women to reclaim their agency and uplift one another.
